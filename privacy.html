Privacy Policy — CareerCraft

Last updated: 2026

CareerCraft (“we”, “our”, “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website and AI cover letter generator.

1. Information you provide

CareerCraft allows users to enter text such as:

  • Job title
  • Job description
  • Skills
  • Edits to generated cover letters
  • Feedback comments

You are free to use the app without creating an account. We do not require personal information, but users may voluntarily include personal data in the text they enter.

2. How your data is used

The text you enter is used only to generate AI content and improve the functionality of the app. It may be processed temporarily by our server and sent to AI service providers to generate results. The generated content is returned to you instantly.

3. Data storage

CareerCraft does not store or save generated cover letters, user input text, or personal information. All content is processed temporarily and then discarded. We do not maintain user accounts or databases in the MVP version.

4. Third-party services

To generate cover letters, CareerCraft uses third-party AI providers. The text you enter may be securely transmitted to these providers for processing.

5. Cookies & tracking

CareerCraft currently does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking. Future versions may introduce analytics and this policy will be updated.

6. User responsibility

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into the generator. You are responsible for reviewing and editing any generated content before using it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CareerCraft evolves. Updates will be posted on this page.

8. Contact
